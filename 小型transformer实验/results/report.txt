Interpretation
--------------
The unmasked model has a lower held-out loss, but the target token is
present in its input at the next position.  Its low loss is therefore
not a valid next-token result: it is copying from the future.  The
causal model cannot see the future; its loss is compared with the
theoretical Bayes loss above.  The next-token attention diagnostic is
the mechanism check: it is exactly zero for the causal model and
positive for the unmasked model.

Position control: unmasked loss on positions 0..T-2 = 0.0020, on the last position = 1.6701; causal = 1.4821 / 1.5604.
